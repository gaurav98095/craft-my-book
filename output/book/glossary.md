# Glossary

**bottleneck** — The bottleneck therefore defines a requirement for the next architecture.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01.*

**final state** — The final state therefore acts as a compact representation of everything the RNN has processed.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01.*

**hidden state** — At every position, the hidden state contains the information the network has chosen to carry forward from the prefix seen so far.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01.*

**initial state** — The model begins with an initial state, often a vector of zeros.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01.*

**recurrent transition** — Information needed at the end must pass through every intermediate recurrent transition, and each transition can weaken or overwrite it.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01. Also discussed in sec_01_01.*

**RNN** — An RNN processes a sequence in order, one token at a time.  
*Introduced in sec_01_01.*
